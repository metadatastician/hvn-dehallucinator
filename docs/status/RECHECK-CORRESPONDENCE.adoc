= Recheck correspondence — Haruspex in Rocq and Lean
:revdate: 2026-11-09
:toc: preamble

The record standards PROOF-POLICY §2 and §2.2 ask for. It covers the
Haruspex theory theorems, `verification/proofs/agda/Haruspex.agda` (module
`Haruspex.Check`) and `verification/proofs/agda/HaruspexExplorer.agda`, and
their recheck legs:

* Rocq: `verification/proofs/coq/Haruspex.v`;
* Lean: `verification/proofs/lean4/Haruspex.lean`.

Both legs are *re-proved*. No Agda → Dedukti transport was tried. The pinned
Lambdapi release has no Lean exporter, so a transported Lean leg was not
possible. A transported Rocq leg was not attempted.

Each re-proof restates the Agda definitions clause for clause, and the
residual-evidence-types Core they rest on, at
`97244f5cb63bd6697caff261b187b62e9788b692`. Each theorem is then stated with
the same hypotheses and conclusion. `scripts/check-recheck-legs.sh` checks
both legs, and fails on any axiom, `sorry` or admitted lemma. Its negative
controls are in `tests/proofs/recheck_legs_test.sh`.

== Translation

The same Agda construct is always rendered the same way.

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
| Agda | Rocq | Lean

| `Set` (the sort of data and of evidence)
| `Type`; `Prop` statements enter by cumulativity
| `Type`; `Prop` statements enter `Type`-valued contexts by `PLift` (Lean has no cumulativity)

| `_≡_`
| `eq` (in `Prop`)
| `Eq` (in `Prop`)

| `Σ`, `_×_`, `_⊎_`, `⊥`, `¬ A`
| `sigT`, `prod`, `sum`, `False`, `A -> False`
| `Sigma` or `PSigma`, `Prod`, `PProd` or `And`, `PSum`, `False`, `A → False`

| `_∈_` (`here`, `there`), proof-relevant
| inductive `mem` in `Type`, same constructors
| inductive `Mem` in `Type`, same constructors

| `Candidate`, `Case` (`inhabited`, `witness`), `Holds`, `Identified`, `Refuted`, `Disagreeing`, `Ctx`, `⟦_⟧`
| the same names; `⟦_⟧` is `denote`
| the same names; `⟦_⟧` is `denote`; `Holds` is polymorphic in the sort of its predicate, as Agda's is in its level

| `_∧_`, `_∨_`, `not` of `Finite.Checker`
| `andb`, `orb`, `negb` (the same defining clauses)
| `&&`, `\|\|`, `!` (the same defining clauses)

| `Schema` (`_≟_`, `≟-sound`, `≟-complete`), `Query` (`_==_`, …)
| `Record`, fields `eqb_shown`, `eqb_shown_sound`, … and `eqb_value`, …
| `structure`, fields `eqbShown`, `eqbShown_sound`, … and `eqbValue`, …

| `with e in eq` in `decide`
| a dependent `match … return … = v -> …` applied to `eq_refl`
| `match e : … with`

| `Agda.Builtin.Int` (`pos`, `negsuc`), `Nat` `_==_`, `_<_`
| own `ZInt`, `natEqb`, `natLtb`, with Agda's clauses
| own `ZInt`, `natEqb`, `natLtb`, with Agda's clauses

| `latentsFrom` / `latentsStep` (mutual)
| one `Fixpoint`, `latentsStep` inlined as an `if` on the same test
| the same inlining. A `mutual` pair would be compiled by well-founded recursion, which brings in `propext` and `Quot.sound`

| an identifier with `-`, `∈`, `′`
| `_` for `-`; `∈-candidate` is `mem_candidate`, `candidate-∈` is `candidate_mem`
| as in Rocq

| the reserved word `as` (Rocq)
| the assumption list is named `asm`
| `asm`, for symmetry
|===

== Residual assumptions

None of the three legs rests on an axiom (`--safe`; `Print Assumptions`;
`#print axioms`). What each leg trusts beyond its kernel is the same in kind:

* the restated residual-evidence-types Core and finite checker are what they
  restate. The Agda leg checks the originals at the pin; the other legs check
  the restatements. This record is the bridge between them, and is the part
  that is pending review;
* Rocq's `vm_compute` decides the explorer equations with Rocq's bytecode
  virtual machine, which is part of Rocq's trusted base;
* Lean's `decide +kernel` uses the kernel's own reduction, not compiled code.
