== What it is

The dehallucinator refuses a hallucinated completion *after* the work has been
done. A completion is checked against a measurement, and when the two disagree
the engine prints the blind-spot witness (`engine/Engine.hs`, `WITNESS regress`).
Haruspex runs the same check *before* any tokens are spent, on the first
crossing: from what the author intends to the prompt that is sent.

Every prompt asks a question of a context: a claim to confirm or a value to
report. Haruspex decides, before the prompt is sent, whether the context it will
be given can settle that question. It answers in one of three ways:

[cols="1,3,3",options="header"]
|===
| Output | Meaning | What the author gets

| `Sound(certificate)`
| Every reading of the context consistent with what it shows agrees on the answer.
| The verdict, and the checker's certificate that it follows from the context.

| `Epi(pair)`
| The context is consistent, but two readings of it disagree on what was asked.
| The *witness pair*: "these two readings of your context both fit and disagree on what you asked". Any answer the model gives is a warrant without a soundness map: belief, not knowledge.

| `Empty(witness)`
| No reading of the context answers yes, or the context admits no reading at all.
| A refuting reading. Or, for an inconsistent context, the assumptions that leave it with no reading.
|===

Haruspex makes *no model call*. It inspects no model and makes no claim about
LLM internals. It runs a total function over a declared, finite schema of the
context, exactly as the certified finite checker of residual-evidence-types
does.

== The check, in residual-evidence terms

Haruspex instantiates the same Core that `verification/proofs/agda/Fibre.agda`
instantiates for the engine (residual-evidence-types, pinned by
`scripts/fetch-siblings.sh`):

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
| Core | Engine (`Fibre.agda`) | Haruspex

| world `W` | recorded task state × surviving count | one reading of the context: a value for every field of the context schema
| observation `observe : W → O`, `r` | the recorded state | what the context actually shows (the fields the prompt will carry)
| evidence `E` (a `Ctx W`) | `Unmeasured` / `Measured m` | the assumptions the prompt states, as decidable predicates on readings
| claim `P` / query `q` | `Complete` | the claim the prompt asks the model to confirm, or the value it asks it to report
| `Case observe r ⟦ Γ ⟧` | `unmeasured s`, `measured s m` | at least one reading fits the context and its assumptions
|===

The outputs are the Core's verdicts. The certified finite checker of
residual-evidence-types decides them for its one fixed model, and
`ResidualEvidence.Finite.Soundness` proves them sound there. `Haruspex.Check`
decides the same verdicts for any declared schema and proves one lemma per
verdict, with the same names, stated in the same Core vocabulary (`Candidate`,
`Case`, `Holds`, `Identified`, and `Refuted` / `Disagreeing` from `Revision`):

[cols="1,2,3",options="header"]
|===
| Verdict | Haruspex output | Lemma in `Haruspex.Check`

| `entailed` | `Sound` | `entailed-sound`: every candidate satisfies the claim (`Holds c (Yes P)`); `entailed-complete` is the converse
| `identified` | `Sound` | `identified-sound`: every candidate has the one value (`Identified c q`); `identified-complete` is the converse
| `unresolved` | `Epi(pair)` | `unresolved-sound`: a supporting and a counterexample candidate both exist
| `unidentified` | `Epi(pair)` | `unidentified-sound`: two candidates disagree on the value (`Disagreeing`); `unidentified-refutes`: `¬ Identified` for every case
| `refuted` | `Empty(witness)` | `refuted-sound`: every candidate satisfies the negation; `refuted-refutes`: the refuting reading as positive data (`Refuted`)
| `inconsistent` | `Empty(witness)` | `inconsistent-sound`: the context has no case at all (compare `Presence.conflict-has-no-case`)
| `no-candidate` | `Empty(witness)` | `no-candidate-sound`: a value asked of a context with no case at all
|===

The generic lemmas do not reduce to the certified checker's own, which are
fixed to its integer worlds. The tie is `HaruspexExplorer`: the certified
checker's model, declared as a Haruspex schema, gives the same candidates in the
same order (`candidates-match`), the same verdicts (`presence-match`,
`identity-match`) and the outputs this table predicts (`outputs-match`), on all
546 configurations by `refl`. Its candidates are the certified checker's
`FiniteCandidate`s and back (`to-finite` / `from-finite`), so the certified
lemmas apply to them as well.

`Epi` is meant in the sense of epistemic-types at `6a68de3` (`Warrant.agda`).
An `Epi κ A` packages a warrant with a token and does not yield `A`. Only a
`SoundWarrant` carries the map from evidence to claim, and
`no-receipt-reflection` (`Applications/RapidNJSkip.agda`) shows a token alone is
never the claim. A model's answer
to an `Epi` prompt is such a token. A `Sound` verdict is a checker certificate
with its soundness lemma, which is the missing map.

Applying any verdict to the *actual* context needs every assumption of the
evidence supplied explicitly (`finite-actual-sound` there, `actual-sound`
here). The schema's enumeration is the first assumption (`Listed`), as the
certified checker's loop bounds are (`InRange`): a candidate is a reading the
schema lists, and nothing transfers to a reading the schema does not list. For
a schema that proves its listing complete, `listing-complete-sound` discharges
that premise once. The prompt's assumptions travel with it, so a verdict never
drops one silently.

== Interface

The interface is the finite checker's, generalised from its fixed explorer
model to a declared schema. It is checked, in
`verification/proofs/agda/Haruspex.agda`:

[source,agda]
----
-- reused from residual-evidence-types (by pin): Candidate, Case, Holds,
-- Identified, Refuted, Disagreeing, Ctx, ⟦_⟧, Finite.Row.Verdict,
-- Finite.Row.IdStatus, Finite.Checker.classify.

record Schema : Set₁ where          -- the context, declared, finite
  field
    Reading    : Set
    readings   : List Reading        -- the declared enumeration
    Shown      : Set
    shows      : Reading → Shown     -- the observation
    _≟_        : Shown → Shown → Bool
    ≟-sound    : ∀ a b → (a ≟ b) ≡ true → a ≡ b
    ≟-complete : ∀ a b → a ≡ b → (a ≟ b) ≡ true

data Ask (R : Set) : Set₁ where
  claim : (R → Bool) → Ask R         -- "confirm that ..."
  query : Query R → Ask R            -- "report the value of ...", decidable equality

record Prompt (S : Schema) : Set₁ where
  field
    shown       : Schema.Shown S                    -- what the prompt will carry
    assumptions : List (Schema.Reading S → Bool)    -- Γ, decidable
    ask         : Ask (Schema.Reading S)

-- in module Check S, by the shape of the ask:
data Output r as a : Set where
  sound : Certificate r as a → Output r as a        -- entailed / identified
  epi   : Pair r as a        → Output r as a        -- unresolved / unidentified
  empty : Refutation r as a  → Output r as a        -- refuted / inconsistent / no-candidate

haruspex : (S : Schema) (p : Prompt S) → Output S p  -- total; no model call
----

The four obligations, and what discharges each:

. *Enumeration soundness and completeness*: a reading is a Core candidate for
  `p` exactly when the enumeration produces it: `Check.candidate-∈` and
  `Check.∈-candidate`, the analogues of `enumerate-complete` and
  `enumerate-sound`.
. *Verdict soundness*: one lemma per row of the verdict table above, in
  `Haruspex.Check`, with completeness for `Sound` (`entailed-complete`,
  `identified-complete`): when a certificate exists, Haruspex gives it.
. *A correspondence table*: `HaruspexExplorer`, by `refl` on all 546
  configurations of the certified checker's model (candidates, both verdicts,
  and the outputs). Mutating the declared schema (dropping the zero assumption,
  or tightening the noise bound by one) is refused by the conversions, and by
  `candidates-match` on its own when the conversions are taken out (run by
  hand, 2026-10-27).
. *Reject fixtures*: `tests/reject/SoundForEpi.agda` claims a `Sound`
  certificate for the Incident's `Epi` prompt; `tests/reject/EpiForInconsistent.agda`
  claims an `Epi` pair for its inconsistent one. The kernel gate requires each
  to be refused with a type error at its own declaration.

Per the estate rulings, the certified checker is Agda (`--safe --without-K`).
Any runtime harness that feeds it schemas belongs to the Idris2 layer, and
none exists yet (HD-H-R). No Python, and JavaScript only under Bun.

== Worked example: the Incident as a pre-flight check

Suppose the author sends, on 2026-08-23, the prompt "confirm the migration is
COMPLETE", with the context the agent had: `state/state.txt` reading
`16 / 16 / 0 / COMPLETE` and no measurement.

This example is checked: `verification/proofs/agda/HaruspexIncident.agda`.

* Schema: readings are (recorded state, surviving count). The author declares
  the records the agent could hold (16/16/0/`COMPLETE`, or the 16/0/1/`RUNNING`
  the engine writes on the measurement) and the counts that could remain (none,
  or 15272). The context shows the recorded state; there are no assumptions;
  the claim is "nothing remains".
* Haruspex finds the reading `(16/16/0/COMPLETE, 0)` and the reading
  `(16/16/0/COMPLETE, 15272)`. Both fit and they disagree on the claim, so the
  output is `Epi(pair)` (`confirm-is-epi`). Read as Fibre candidates under the
  record alone, they are the readings of `Fibre.incident-witness`
  (`same-readings-as-the-engine`): the pair the engine now prints after the
  fact, delivered before the prompt is sent.
* Add the measurement to the context (remaining is 15272) and ask again: every
  candidate refutes the claim, and the output is `Empty`, with the refuting
  reading `(16/16/0/COMPLETE, 15272)` (`refuted-is-empty`, `refuting-reading`).
* Assert "done" on top of the measurement: no reading fits the record, the
  measurement and the claim together, and the output is `Empty` with no case
  at all (`asserted-has-no-case`; compare `Fibre.hallucinated-complete-has-no-case`).
* Ask instead "how many artefacts remain?" with the measurement in the context.
  The output is `Sound`, with value 15272 (`how-many-is-sound`, `how-many-value`).

[[as-the-proofs-progress]]
== As the proofs progress

* `Composition.coarsen-claim`: a claim at a coarse observation holds at the
  fine one, not the converse. That becomes Haruspex's "you asked a finer question
  than the context supports". When a claim holds only at a finer observation
  than the context shows, the output is `Epi` with the coarsening named.
* `Context.no-thinning-into-empty`: a non-empty context never thins into the
  empty one. That becomes "a prompt with assumptions cannot be answered from an
  empty context", and the check names which assumptions.
* echo-types' order-type fidelity is OPEN and is *not* relied on here.

== Decisions (owner, 2026-10-14)

. *Owning organisation: metadatastician.* Haruspex lives beside the engine it
  extends. It consumes the hyperpolymath proof siblings (residual-evidence-types,
  epistemic-types) by pin, as `Fibre.agda` does.
. *Three outputs, kept.* HDH-01 said a prompt whose claim has an empty fibre
  "gets the witness pair". In the types, the disagreeing pair witnesses
  *non-identification* (`unresolved` / `unidentified`). Emptiness is witnessed
  by a refutation (`refuted`) or by no case at all (`inconsistent`). The two
  stay apart as `Epi(pair)` and `Empty(witness)`, and the output has three
  cases, not two.

[[open-questions]]
== Open questions

. *Where the schema comes from.* Haruspex checks a declared schema; it does not
  read free text, which would need a model. Who declares the schema for a prompt
  (the author, a tool, a template library) is outside this design.
. *The runtime harness (HD-H-R, OPEN).* The checker is a total Agda function,
  checked by normalisation. Nothing yet runs it on a schema supplied at run
  time, or reports its output to an author. That harness belongs to the Idris2
  layer.

== Out of scope

Any claim about LLM internals; extracting a schema from a natural-language
prompt.
